What a printer actually does, and which of your modeling decisions it will refuse.

## What it covers

- Manifold checking — watertight, no self-intersection, normals outward
- Overhangs and the 45° rule; orientation as the only lever
- Layer height, infill, walls, supports — the four settings that matter
- Slicing: a solid intersected by a stack of planes, ordered into moves
- Sending a file to the FabLab queue
