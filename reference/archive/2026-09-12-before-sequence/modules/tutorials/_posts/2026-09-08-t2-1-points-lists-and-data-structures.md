A point is three numbers in order. Everything in this course is that idea, repeated.

## What it covers

- A point as `[x, y, z]` — coordinates and data types
- Many points are a **list**: `Index`, `Domain Size`, length
- **Append** and **pop**; selecting a single item; mixing two lists
- **Graft** and **flatten** — changing list *structure* without touching contents
- The Spreadsheet editor as the list made visible: one row per element
