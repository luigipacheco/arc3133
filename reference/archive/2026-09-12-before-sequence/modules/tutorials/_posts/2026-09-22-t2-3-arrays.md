One node operating on every element of a collection *is* a loop. Nothing on screen will tell you so.

## What it covers

- The loop, hiding in plain sight; a grid as a **nested loop**
- `Switch` and `Compare` as the **if statement** — filtering a collection
- Cartesian · radial · hexagonal · along a curve
- How element order determines the order of everything downstream
